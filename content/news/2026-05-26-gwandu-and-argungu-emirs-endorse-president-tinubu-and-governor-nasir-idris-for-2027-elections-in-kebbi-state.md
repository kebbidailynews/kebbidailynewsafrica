  **Background on Kebbi State's Political Landscape in 2026**


  Kebbi State, located in Nigeria's northwest, plays a strategic role in national politics due to its agricultural importance, border dynamics, and history of shifting alliances. Governor Nasir Idris, a former unionist and educationist, assumed office in 2023 and has focused on infrastructure, security, and agriculture-led development. His administration's alignment with the federal government under President Tinubu has been consistent, particularly in areas like the Renewed Hope Agenda.


  Recent APC activities in the state, including direct primaries and endorsements, show the party consolidating around Idris and Tinubu. The governor's visits to the palaces were not isolated events but part of broader consultations following his formal endorsement as the APC governorship flag bearer. He was accompanied by key figures including Minister of Budget and Economic Planning Senator Abubakar Atiku Bagudu, former Governor Ambassador Sa’idu Usman Nasamu Dakingari, and other APC leaders.


  This timing is crucial. With 2027 elections approaching, opposition parties like the African Democratic Congress (ADC) are positioning alternatives, including former Attorney General Abubakar Malami as a potential governorship contender. The royal endorsements serve as a counter to such moves, reinforcing APC dominance in key emirates.


  **The Emir of Gwandu's Strong Endorsement for Continuity**


  At his palace in Birnin Kebbi, the Emir of Gwandu and Chairman of the Kebbi State Council of Chiefs, His Royal Highness Alhaji Muhammadu Ilyasu Bashar, spoke warmly about the governor's qualities. He highlighted humility, patience, and commitment to the people's welfare as reasons the traditional institution stands firmly with Idris and Tinubu.


  The Emir recalled his long relationships with national leaders, including former President Muhammadu Buhari, and stressed that effective leadership rests on trust and closeness to the people. He assured the governor of continued prayers and support from the traditional institution for both state and federal leadership.


  Governor Idris responded by describing the palace as a spiritual and political reference point. He noted historical visits by past leaders seeking blessings before assuming office, including Presidents Buhari and Tinubu. The governor linked federal projects in Kebbi to the need for continuity, arguing that a second term for Tinubu would sustain ongoing developments and deliver more dividends of democracy.


  **Emir of Argungu Praises Infrastructural Gains**


  The visit to the Emir of Argungu, His Royal Highness Alhaji Sama’ila Muhammadu Mera, yielded equally supportive remarks. The Emir commended Governor Idris for massive infrastructural projects and people-oriented programs, particularly within the Argungu Emirate. He praised President Tinubu for national initiatives such as the Sokoto-Badagry Super Highway, describing him as a leader focused on growth and unity.


  The Emir reaffirmed total support for both leaders ahead of 2027 and offered prayers for peace and progress. Governor Idris appreciated the consistent backing from the Emirate and reiterated the APC's resolve to back Tinubu for a second term due to federal interventions in the state.


  These endorsements carry symbolic weight in a region where traditional rulers influence voter sentiment significantly. Analysts note that such blessings often translate into grassroots mobilization during elections.

  **Strategic Implications for 2027 Elections**


  The Kebbi endorsements fit into President Tinubu's broader re-election strategy. Northern support remains critical for APC victory. Governor Idris's promise to deliver the state aligns with similar assurances from other APC governors. The involvement of heavyweights like Senator Atiku Bagudu strengthens this front.


  Opposition dynamics add intrigue. Abubakar Malami's move to ADC and governorship ambitions create potential flashpoints. His focus on insecurity, education, and agriculture directly challenges Idris's record. However, the solid APC front in Kebbi, backed by emirs, may limit opposition inroads.


  Economic and developmental angles matter. Kebbi benefits from federal projects in agriculture, roads, and power. Governor Idris has emphasized complementing these efforts. Success here could serve as a model for other states in demonstrating "continuity" as a campaign theme.


  **The Role of Traditional Institutions in Modern Nigerian Politics**


  Nigeria's traditional rulers remain influential despite constitutional limits on their political roles. In Kebbi, as in many northern states, emirs command respect that politicians court actively. This dynamic blends spiritual authority with political pragmatism.


  Critics argue such visits risk blurring lines between state and traditional spheres. Supporters counter that seeking blessings fosters cultural harmony and stability. The 2027 cycle will test how effectively these alliances translate into votes amid economic pressures and opposition campaigns.

  Opportunities include leveraging federal resources for visible projects. The Argungu Fishing Festival and other cultural events offer platforms for further outreach. APC's early consolidation, including sole candidacy for Idris, minimizes internal primaries drama.
